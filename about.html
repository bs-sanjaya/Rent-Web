About UVA Car Rent

We started with one vision.

In 2024, we set out to create a better vehicle rental experience. UVA Car Rent exists to make sure every customer enjoys transparent pricing, quality vehicles, and exceptional service.

Our mission

Make renting a car feel like it’s already yours.

At UVA Car Rent, we measure everything on three things: how fast you get the keys, how well the car matches what you booked, and how transparent the final price is. Every decision we make is shaped by these standards.That’s why we built our own inspection checklist, trained every location to the same process, and keep all insurance and fees clear and upfront—no surprises at the counter.
